Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data AW Webdesign collects when you use this website or contact me, why it is collected, and the rights you have over it. It is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Belgian data protection law.

01Who is responsible

The person responsible for your data (the "data controller") is Axel Willockx, trading as AW Webdesign, based in Belgium. For any question about your data or this policy, contact axelwillockx@icloud.com.

02What data I collect

  • Contact details you give me through the contact form or by email: your name, email address, phone number (if you share it), the package you are interested in, and the content of your message.
  • Technical data automatically recorded by the hosting server: your IP address, browser type, device and the date and time of your visit. This is standard server logging used to keep the site running and secure.
  • This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies and does not build a profile of you.

03Why I collect it and the legal basis

  • To answer your message and prepare a possible project — legal basis: your consent and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To keep the website secure and working — legal basis: my legitimate interest in a safe, functioning site.

04Who your data is shared with

Your data is never sold. It is only shared with the service providers needed to run the site and reply to you, such as the hosting and email provider. These providers process data on my instructions and are bound to protect it. Data is kept within the EU/EEA wherever possible; if a provider processes data outside the EEA, appropriate safeguards (such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses) are in place.

05How long it is kept

Messages and contact details are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and, if we work together, for the duration of the project plus any legal retention period. After that they are deleted. Server logs are kept for a short period for security purposes and then removed.

06Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to how your data is used;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, email axelwillockx@icloud.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données) at gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

07Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with a new "last updated" date.
