Case Study

Picture Perfect.

A bold black-and-white portfolio for a Belgian photographer, built to let the images carry the whole experience and turn visitors into bookings.

Year2026
RoleDesign & Development
StackHTML, CSS, JavaScript, GSAP
Visit website ↗

The brief

A photographer lives or dies by their images, so the website had to get out of the way. The brief was a portfolio that feels editorial and confident, puts the work first, and makes booking a shoot effortless.

The look

Strict black and white, oversized display type and a split-screen hero: brand on one side, a striking photograph on the other. The high-contrast palette keeps the focus on the photography while giving the whole site a gallery-like feel.

Motion & flow

Smooth, inertia-based scrolling ties the sections together, images reveal as they enter the frame, and the layout guides the eye from the hero straight into the gallery. A persistent booking call to action means the visitor is never more than one tap from getting in touch.

The result

A portfolio that looks like a magazine spread, loads fast, and reads beautifully on a phone. The photography stays the star, and the clear booking flow turns curious visitors into real clients.
