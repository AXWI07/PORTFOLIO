Case Study

Crombruggen.

A dark, cinematic one-pager for a photographer and video maker. Built to feel like a film opening: a slow reveal, then the work.

Year2026
RoleDesign & Development
StackHTML, CSS, JavaScript, GSAP
Visit website

The brief

Crombruggen shoots both photography and video, so the site had to carry two disciplines without feeling split in half. The goal: one page, one atmosphere, and a first impression strong enough that visitors keep scrolling instead of bouncing.

The opening

The site opens on near black with nothing but the handwritten Crombruggen logo and a single scroll cue. It holds for a beat before releasing you into the page. That restraint sets the tone for everything after it: patient, cinematic and clearly about image making.

Motion with a purpose

Instead of decorating the page with effects, the motion does the storytelling. A marquee pushes the two disciplines past the viewport, and the opening statement lights up word by word as you scroll, so reading it becomes an action rather than a paragraph you skip. Everything is tied to scroll position and smoothed, which keeps it deliberate instead of restless.

Dark to light

After the cinematic opening the page flips to a clean, light canvas for the services. Numbered chapters give the content an obvious rhythm, and the contrast makes the photography pop off the page. It also gives the visitor a moment to breathe before the next section arrives.

The result

A site that feels like a showreel rather than a brochure. It is hand coded, so it loads fast and stays sharp on every screen, and it gives Crombruggen a presence online that matches the work: calm, atmospheric and confident.
