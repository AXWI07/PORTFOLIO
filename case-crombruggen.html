Case Study

Crombruggen.

A quiet, editorial one-pager for a Belgian estate, built around a camera-aperture intro and a distortion-mask hero that reacts as you move.

Year2026
RoleDesign & Development
StackHTML, CSS, JavaScript, GSAP
Visit website ↗

The brief

Crombruggen needed a website that felt as calm and considered as the place itself. No loud sales pitch, no template look. The goal was a single, flowing page that reads like a printed magazine and leaves visitors with one clear feeling before they reach out.

The look

I built the design around a soft, paper-toned palette and a single expressive display type. Generous whitespace does most of the work: images breathe, headlines get room, and nothing competes for attention. The result is restraint that reads as quality.

Motion

The page opens with a camera aperture that irises open into the hero, tying the whole site to the idea of framing a view. From there, a distortion mask lets the landscape bleed through a soft edge that shifts subtly with the cursor. Every animation is scroll-linked and smoothed, so the movement feels intentional rather than decorative.

The result

A site that feels premium the moment it loads, loads fast because it is hand-coded, and works on every screen. It gives Crombruggen a digital presence that matches the estate: understated, confident and memorable.
